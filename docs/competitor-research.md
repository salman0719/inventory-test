# Competitor and Market Research

## Research rules

Use material clearly available to the public:

- Product and pricing pages.
- Public help centres and user guides.
- Public API documentation.
- Public videos and webinars.
- Public reviews and discussions, treating individual opinions as anecdotal.

Do not scrape private or authenticated areas, bypass access controls, reverse engineer software, or copy proprietary content/designs.

### Zoho caution

Zoho's Terms of Service include a restriction on using Zoho Services for competitive or benchmarking purposes. Do not systematically explore or use Zoho's demo service for competitor benchmarking unless the user has obtained written permission from Zoho. This is not only an automated-scraping restriction.

Public Zoho marketing pages and public documentation may be reviewed for general product-domain understanding, subject to their terms. Relevant public starting points:

- https://www.zoho.com/au/inventory/
- https://www.zoho.com/inventory/help/
- https://www.zoho.com/inventory/api/v1/introduction/
- https://www.zoho.com/inventory/api/v1/items/
- https://www.zoho.com/inventory/api/v1/oauth/

Do not assume a public demo is free of contractual restrictions.

## Research log

For each source record:

- Product/company.
- URL.
- Date accessed.
- Type of source.
- Specific observations.
- What the source supports.
- What remains an assumption.
- Relevance to an Australian small business.

## Questions to answer

1. What are the minimum inventory workflows customers expect?
2. How do products, variants, units, and SKUs commonly work?
3. How do purchase orders and receiving work, especially partial deliveries?
4. What terminology is familiar to cafe and retail operators?
5. What is typically too complex for a small operator?
6. Which features are table stakes versus differentiators?
7. What are common onboarding and migration pain points?
8. What pricing models are visible in the Australian market?
