## Suggested cafe demonstration

1. Open Demo Cafe dashboard.
2. Point out low chicken or dairy stock.
3. Open the product and review movement history.
4. Create a purchase order for a fictional supplier.
5. Receive only part of the order.
6. Show updated stock and remaining quantity.
7. Receive the balance.
8. Record wastage or a stock count correction.
9. Review the movement history and dashboard.

## Suggested retail demonstration

1. Open Demo Retail.
2. Search for a product by name or SKU.
3. Review current stock and reorder threshold.
4. Create a purchase order.
5. Receive a partial delivery.
6. Review low-stock report and movement history.
7. Switch to Demo Cafe to demonstrate the separate business dataset.

## Interview questions

- How do you currently manage inventory?
- What is the most frustrating part?
- What do you use to create and track supplier orders?
- How often do deliveries arrive partially or differ from the order?
- What happens when stock counts do not match?
- What would make you consider changing your current process?
- What would stop you from switching?
- If this solved the problem, would you be willing to trial it?
- What would you expect to pay, and how would you prefer to pay?
